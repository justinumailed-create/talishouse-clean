04 · Parallel campaign design

Bot vs agents: same markets, fair split, shared learning

Aritri and Enanko keep calling as people, not as bot operators. Bot and team work side by side on matched lists, so by 19 December the numbers show what works.

How the list is split

Fairness rules

  • Same area codes, same lead age, same SMS teaser beforehand
  • Same calling hours and same follow-up rules
  • Every registration carries an agent code (bot has its own), so attribution is clean
  • Volume-adjusted: we compare rates and cost per result, not raw totals

The learning loop runs both ways

Weekly rhythm: score calls on Thursday → update the bot and run the training huddle on Friday → relaunch on Monday.

09 · Market-partner network · as you described it

One investor partner for each of the 45 areas

A licence, not a franchise. One investor-type partner owns one of the 45 areas (each an 80 km market). We start once the bot is making its first sales.

The model

  • Who: an investor with vision who sees the business we've built. Not a broker, not an agent.
  • What they buy: the licence for one area, roughly CA$100k depending on population your estimate; per-area figure to confirm
  • When: after the bot makes its first sales
  • Who sells it: Arun sources and opens, Ralf closes. Aritri and Enanko don't sell licences.
  • What they get: a share of everything generated in their area, for as long as they hold the licence
